# Karnataka Crosswalk First-Pass Review

**Review date:** 2026-10-21  
**Status:** Review-only; no crosswalk changes or approvals made.

## Scope and method

This first pass checks the 13 Karnataka crop labels listed for alias or historical-name review in the existing Karnataka review artifacts against:

- `data/All_Districts_Merged.xlsx`, sheet `Districts`.
- `data/weather_location_resolution/lgd_district_snapshot_karnataka.csv`.
- `data/weather_location_resolution/karnataka_lgd_crosswalk_audit.json` and the Karnataka entries in `data/weather_location_resolution/merged_lgd_unresolved_review_by_state.md`.

The workbook and snapshot establish the current record's state, code, name, version, and available source-record metadata. They do not establish that an old crop label historically refers to that record. The raw `KARNATAKA 2.xlsx` cited in the snapshot/audit was not available for this review. No government notification, gazette, district history, or other primary evidence connecting the old crop labels to current identities was located in these materials.

Name similarity and the candidate suggestions in the unresolved review are not treated as identity evidence. “Plausible candidate” below is a search/review classification only, not a verified alias or approved crosswalk.

## Exact matches, plausible candidates, and unresolved decisions

### Exact matches

None of the 13 proposed old labels exactly matches its candidate current English name after case and whitespace normalization. The exact-name findings for other Karnataka crop labels in the prior audit are separate and are not evidence for any of these aliases.

### Plausible historical or spelling/name candidates

The candidate pairs in the table are suitable for further documentary checking because they are the proposed/current-name pairings considered in this first pass. The workbook and snapshot support the existence and Karnataka identity of each **current** record only. They do not prove a renaming, transliteration, spelling transition, or identity continuity for any old label.

### Unresolved cases

All 13 mapping decisions remain unresolved. In particular, the existing unresolved-review candidate list for `GULBARGA` does not identify `Kalaburagi` among its displayed suggestions; the current workbook and snapshot show `Kalaburagi` with code `538`, but that alone does not establish the alias. It should be reviewed against authoritative historical evidence, not accepted from the name/code match alone.

## Evidence needed before approval

For each proposed link, obtain an authoritative source that explicitly connects the old label to the current Karnataka district identity for the relevant period, such as an official renaming notification, gazette, district-government history, or another primary government record. Capture the evidence URL, date, relevant text/record, reviewer, and rationale in a review artifact. Also recover the cited original LGD export or obtain a fresh official export to independently validate the current record and its provenance.

Until that review is complete, keep all 13 mappings pending. This report does not modify or approve the operational crosswalk and does not affect the crop dataset, weather registry, coordinates, models, or APIs.
